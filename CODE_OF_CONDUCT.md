# Code of conduct

PlanLens welcomes questions, bug reports and contributions from anyone willing to work constructively. This
code applies to the repository's issues, pull requests and other project spaces, and to anyone representing the
project elsewhere.

## Expected

- Be respectful, and assume good faith.
- Keep discussion on the work. Critique code, plans and ideas, not people.
- Give and accept review plainly. Say what you observed and what you expected.
- Credit other people's work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexual language, imagery or attention.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an email address, without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Report a concern

Send a private message to the maintainer, Nurdaulet Abdrakhman, on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nurdaulet-abdrakhman-523784375). Include links to the content and the
people involved. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is shared with no one without
permission.

If the concern is about the maintainer, or you prefer not to contact them, report the content to GitHub:
[Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people from the repository.
The response fits the behaviour: usually a private warning first, and an immediate block for threats or
harassment.
